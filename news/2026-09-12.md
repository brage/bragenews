## AI News

- **[Anthropic Launches Claude Fable 5.1](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/01/anthropic-claude-fable-5-1/)** — Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.1 on September 1, 2026, with improved performance and significantly reduced costs. The model maintains Fable 5's input/output pricing at $10/$50 per million tokens but reduces cache-read costs by 75% to $0.25 per million tokens, making it ~25% cheaper for typical workloads and up to 45% cheaper for agentic work. The model supports 1M token context window with 128K max output tokens.

- **[Claude Fable 5.1 Available Across Cloud Platforms](https://llm-stats.com/models/claude-fable-5-1)** — Claude Fable 5.1 (claude-fable-5-1) is now generally available on Claude API, Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure. The model achieves similar or better results than Fable 5 at low/medium effort levels with much higher performance at higher effort tiers, outperforming Fable 5, Opus 5, and OpenAI GPT-5.6 Sol on multiple benchmarks.

- **[OpenAI GPT-6 Astra Reaches Public Availability](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/03/open-ai-astra-gpt-6-cyber.html)** — GPT-6 Astra, released September 3-4, 2026, represents a generational leap for cybersecurity, professional work, software engineering, and science. OpenAI trained the model using its largest training run to date with over 100,000 GPUs at its Stargate site in Texas. The company's research leadership claims it could eventually be seen as the arrival of AGI.
