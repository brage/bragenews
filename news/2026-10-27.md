## AI News

- **[Claude Haiku 5.5 Released (October 7, 2026)](https://gori.me/it/171039)** — Anthropic announced Claude Haiku 5.5 on October 7, 2026, as the fastest and most cost-effective small model in the Claude family. Pricing for prompts up to 100,000 tokens is ~$0.10 per million input tokens and ~$0.50 per million output tokens, roughly 90% cheaper than Haiku 4.5. On October 29, Haiku 4.5 will be removed from Chat, Cowork, and Design model selectors, with Haiku 5.5 as its replacement. The older model remains available in Claude Code, Team, and Enterprise plans.

- **[OpenAI Continues GPT-5.6 Rollout (July 2026)](https://letsdatascience.com/news/openai-releases-gpt-56-and-chatgpt-work-025f6cb2)** — OpenAI released GPT-5.6 on July 9, 2026, alongside ChatGPT Work, extending the Sol, Terra, and Luna family beyond preview. Sol serves as the flagship model, Terra covers balanced professional tasks, and Luna provides the fastest, most cost-effective option. Two performance modes, "max" (extended reasoning) and "ultra" (parallel multi-agent), support diverse workloads.
